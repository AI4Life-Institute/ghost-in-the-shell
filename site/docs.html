多个微信，共享一个 ClaudeMultiple WeChat accounts, one Claude

家人、朋友、团队成员各用自己的微信，同时连到同一台小鬼，共用一个 Claude Pro/Max。发送 /share 生成邀请码，扫码即加入。

Family, friends, or teammates each use their own WeChat, all connected to the same Ghost and sharing one Claude Pro/Max subscription. Send /share to get an invite code.

语音 · 图片Voice · Images

直接发图给小鬼，Claude 会看图回答——截图、拍照都行。发语音也可以，小鬼会把转写内容转给 Claude。建议开启微信的语音转文字效果更好。

Send images directly in WeChat and Claude will analyze them — screenshots, photos, anything. Voice messages work too: Ghost forwards the transcription to Claude. Enable WeChat's voice-to-text for best results.
